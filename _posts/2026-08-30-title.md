- Online Friends

 A thrilling alternative to real and physical connections, meaningful bonds can be made and bare social needs can be met. It does have it's limitations depending on the individual, if this is your only source of friendship you may feel insecure when the other person goes out to spend time with their irl friends! As valuable as your time may be to them, nothing can replace real life friendships.. Now, if you are able to meet an individual in similar circumstances as you are, your bond may be way more stronger and this usually ends up being your closest friends. Misery is a glue.
